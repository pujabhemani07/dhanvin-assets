Child Education Planning

About This Service

Secure Your Child's Dream Education

Education costs in India are rising at 10-12% annually. An engineering degree that costs ₹15 lakhs today will cost ₹50+ lakhs in 15 years. Without a dedicated education fund, this can derail your finances entirely.

We design goal-based child education portfolios combining equity mutual funds, Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (for girl children) and debt funds to ensure the money is ready exactly when your child needs it — with inflation-beating returns.
