Tax Saving Tools

Popular Tax-Saving Instruments

ELSS Mutual Funds

Best 80C instrument: 3-year lock-in, equity returns, up to ₹1.5L deduction. Ideal for wealth creation.

PPF (Public Provident Fund)

Risk-free, government-backed savings with 7.1% interest and EEE tax status (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt).

NPS (National Pension System)

Additional ₹50,000 deduction under 80CCD(1B) over and above 80C limit. Great for retirement planning.

Health Insurance (80D)

Deduction up to ₹25,000 for self/family and ₹50,000 for senior citizen parents under Section 80D.
