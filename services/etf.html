About This Service

Understand exchange-traded funds

Exchange-traded funds (ETFs) are market-traded funds that typically track an index, asset or basket. They can offer market access and diversification, but prices fluctuate and returns are not guaranteed. Product structure, liquidity, costs and tracking difference matter.

Key Considerations

What You Should Know

Index exposure

Understand the benchmark or asset the ETF is designed to track.

Diversification

Review the underlying holdings and concentration before investing.

Trading and liquidity

Consider exchange liquidity, bid–ask spreads and trading hours.

Costs and tracking

Compare expense ratio, tracking difference and other applicable costs.
