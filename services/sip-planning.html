About This Service

What is SIP Planning?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount every month in a mutual fund of your choice. It's the most effective way to build long-term wealth because it harnesses the twin powers of rupee cost averaging and compounding.

At Dhanvin Assets, we don't just set up your SIP — we design a complete SIP strategy aligned to each of your goals: child's education, home purchase, retirement corpus or wealth creation. We also conduct step-up SIP reviews annually to increase your investment in line with income growth.

Why SIP

Benefits of SIP Investing

Rupee Cost Averaging

Buy more units when markets are low and fewer when markets are high — automatically reducing your average cost over time.

Power of Compounding

Your returns earn returns. A ₹5,000/month SIP at 12% for 25 years grows to over ₹94 lakhs on just ₹15 lakh invested.

Financial Discipline

Auto-debit ensures you invest first and spend later — the golden rule of wealth creation made effortless.

Flexible & Scalable

Pause, stop or increase your SIP anytime. Step-up SIP lets you automatically increase investment annually.
