About This Service

What Are Mutual Funds?

A Mutual Fund pools money from thousands of investors to invest in a professionally managed, diversified portfolio of stocks, bonds or other securities. SEBI regulates all mutual funds in India ensuring complete transparency and investor protection.

At Dhanvin Assets, we analyze 500+ mutual fund schemes and recommend only those that align perfectly with your risk profile, time horizon and financial goals. We guide you through equity, debt, hybrid and ELSS fund categories.

Why Invest

Benefits of Mutual Funds

Professional Management

Expert fund managers with decades of experience manage your investments using research-backed strategies.

Diversification

Spread risk across 30-100 stocks/bonds, reducing the impact of any single investment's poor performance.

High Liquidity

Redeem your investments anytime (for open-ended funds) and receive funds within 2-3 business days.

Start with ₹500

Begin your investment journey with as little as ₹500 per month through SIP in most mutual funds.
